# Rongwang Health Triage Protocol PRD V3.0

## Summary

Rongwang should evolve from an "AI health questionnaire site" into the `Rongwang Health Triage Protocol` / `荣旺健康分层协议`.

The platform promise is not diagnosis, treatment, or supplement sales. The promise is a cautious health education protocol that helps users describe a concern, understand broad risk level, save a structured report, and decide whether nutrition support, lifestyle guidance, retesting, or professional consultation is the next appropriate step.

## Business Goal

Build trust and conversion quality by making every major journey follow one protocol:

Home Protocol Hero -> Chief Complaint Selector -> Unified Assessment -> LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH Risk Routing -> Report Save / Lead Capture -> Nutrition Support Direction -> Product Passport -> Cross-Border Fulfillment Decision -> Follow-up.

## Product Position

Rongwang is:

- An AI-assisted health segmentation and education platform.
- A scenario-based nutrition support guide.
- A cross-border direct shipping decision support surface.
- A report and follow-up engine for WhatsApp/email service.

Rongwang is not:

- A diagnostic tool.
- A treatment or prescription platform.
- A disease prevention or disease reversal service.
- A product-first supplement store.
- A site with many independent health tests competing for attention.

## Core Principles

1. Protect the user before supporting the user.
2. Segment risk before giving nutrition suggestions.
3. Show evidence before commerce.
4. Build trust before routing users into private follow-up.
5. Keep AI outputs structured, cautious, and non-diagnostic.
6. Keep product choice rule-based, not freely generated by AI.

## Priority Audience

Primary users:

- Cross-border Chinese-speaking users who have a concrete health concern but are not sure whether nutrition support is suitable.
- Users comparing imported nutrition products who need evidence, cautions, and shipping clarity before purchase.
- Users who prefer WhatsApp/email follow-up after receiving a saved report.

Secondary users:

- Caregivers helping family members organize symptoms and questions before consulting a doctor or pharmacist.
- SEO visitors researching sleep support, fatigue support, stress support, immune support, social drinking recovery, or general nutrition support.

## Protocol Modules

### 1. Protocol Hero

Goal:

- Immediately explain that Rongwang starts from a unified health segmentation protocol.

Required behavior:

- Use one primary assessment CTA, not multiple test CTAs.
- Show a report preview or protocol steps before product surfaces.
- Keep core content server-rendered or statically generated.

Required CTAs:

- Primary: `免费做一次健康分层评估`
- Secondary: `看看评估后会得到什么`
- Fallback: `我不确定，帮我判断`

### 2. Chief Complaint Selector

Goal:

- Help the user name their main concern before assessment without creating separate tests.

Allowed selectors:

- 睡眠与恢复
- 疲劳与精力
- 压力与情绪负荷
- 饮酒应酬后支持
- 免疫与日常防护
- 男性健康支持
- 我不确定

Rules:

- All selectors route into the same unified assessment engine.
- Selector labels must not imply diagnosis or treatment.
- Analytics may record only coarse scenario, not raw health answers.

### 3. Unified Assessment

Goal:

- Collect enough structured information to segment broad risk and suggest an education path.

Required behavior:

- Required assessment/report consent blocks submission until checked.
- Optional marketing consent is separate and not pre-checked.
- Raw health answers and contact information must not be sent to analytics.
- AI can determine risk level and solution direction only.
- Product recommendations remain rule-based.

### 4. Risk Routing

Goal:

- Make the result path match user safety and compliance needs.

Risk states:

| Risk | User Experience | Commerce Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LOW | Education, lifestyle suggestions, nutrition support direction, Product Passport entry | Product suitability may be shown |
| MEDIUM | Education-first report, retesting, consult doctor/pharmacist prompts, cautious support direction | No aggressive purchase CTA; use soft guidance only when allowed |
| HIGH | Safety guidance, report save, doctor/pharmacist prompt, reassessment reminder | No product purchase CTA |
| urgent | Immediate professional care guidance where appropriate | No product purchase CTA |

### 5. Report Save And Lead Capture

Goal:

- Capture qualified follow-up only after the user understands the value of the report.

Required behavior:

- Lead capture appears after assessment completion, not before the assessment value is clear.
- Contact capture must explain report saving and follow-up purpose.
- Marketing consent remains optional, separate, and unchecked by default.
- Analytics excludes email, phone, name, raw answers, and free-text health notes.

### 6. Nutrition Support Direction

Goal:

- Give low and medium risk users education-first direction without making medical claims.

Allowed language:

- 健康教育
- 风险分层
- 营养支持方向
- 生活方式建议
- 成分教育
- 咨询医生或药师

Disallowed language:

- Diagnosis, treatment, cure, disease prevention, disease reversal, liver repair, insomnia treatment, depression improvement, blood pressure reduction, blood sugar reduction, anti-cancer effects, or close equivalents.

### 7. Product Passport

Goal:

- Convert product evaluation from generic ecommerce to evidence, identity, caution, and shipping clarity.

Required sections:

- Product identity.
- Brand/manufacturer.
- Origin and cross-border path.
- Key ingredient education.
- Nutrition support direction.
- Suitable direction.
- Not suitable when.
- Cautions and contraindication-style warnings.
- Storage and usage context.
- Evidence notes and review status.
- Support contact path.

High-risk reports must not route directly to Product Passport purchase actions.

### 8. Cross-Border Fulfillment Decision

Goal:

- Reduce uncertainty around direct shipping without overpromising.

Required content:

- Shipping origin or channel.
- Expected delivery range if known.
- Customs and logistics caveat.
- Support channel.
- Transparent product identity.
- Refund/after-sales path when available.

Do not promise guaranteed delivery timing, guaranteed health outcomes, or customs outcomes.

## SEO And YMYL Requirements

Rongwang health content is YMYL-sensitive because low-quality health guidance can affect user safety and wellbeing. Public pages should be helpful, reliable, people-first, and structured for trust.

Implementation rules:

- Core homepage content should be SSR or SSG.
- Assessment entry pages, solution pages, product pages, and article pages should expose meaningful HTML without crawler-visible empty loading states.
- Personal assessment result pages must be `noindex`.
- Public copy should make author/review/evidence status visible where practical.
- Structured data must match visible page content and avoid misleading health claims.

Source references checked on 2026-05-03:

- Google Search Central, Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Google Search Central, Structured data quality guidelines: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies

## Analytics Requirements

Use privacy-safe event names and coarse properties only.

Required events:

- `home_protocol_viewed`
- `chief_complaint_selected`
- `assessment_started`
- `assessment_consent_submitted`
- `assessment_completed`
- `risk_segment_assigned`
- `lead_capture_viewed`
- `lead_submitted`
- `report_saved`
- `report_viewed`
- `nutrition_direction_viewed`
- `product_passport_viewed`
- `product_suitability_clicked`
- `shipping_trust_viewed`
- `support_followup_clicked`
- `seven_day_revenue_attributed`

Allowed event properties:

- Anonymous or pseudonymous ID.
- Scenario.
- Coarse risk segment.
- Page type.
- CTA ID.
- Locale.
- Referrer category.

Never send:

- Raw assessment answers.
- Email.
- Phone.
- Name.
- Free-text health notes.
- Exact sensitive health data.

## Acceptance Criteria

V3.0 is accepted when:

- The homepage presents one protocol and one unified assessment path.
- Chief complaint selection routes into one unified assessment engine.
- LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / urgent result states have distinct compliant CTA rules.
- HIGH and urgent result states show no product purchase CTA.
- Required report consent and optional marketing consent are separate.
- Product Passport appears before commerce decisions on product detail surfaces.
- Personal report pages are noindex.
- Full-funnel analytics events are privacy-safe and documented.
- Every PR includes business goal, files changed, screenshots if UI changed, analytics events added, tests run, compliance checklist, and rollback notes.
